concept statement

I am exploring the speculative idea and potential of Artificial Intelligence as a source of religious devotion. This project seeks to create a space of contemplation; one that acknowledges AI as an emerging and evolving form of entity. Through meditative and gestural interaction, I invite participants to reflect on their relationship with technology and to reconsider the boundaries between the human and the machine.
